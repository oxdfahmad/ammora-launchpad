Live launches

Configs

Data is read live from GIWA Sepolia via the Ammora SDK. Launches are discovered by scanning LaunchCreated events from the launch factory. Nothing here is a mock.

Create a launch

One atomic transaction: a new Config (derived from the reviewed Ammora template #6), a new token, and your first buy on its Launch Curve. You are the Config owner, the Creator and the first-buy recipient. The SDK plans everything; your wallet signs.

Preview is free and read-only. It checks your live WETH balance and allowance first — if either is short, the plan reports APPROVAL_REQUIRED (exact-amount approval) or a balance error instead of failing on-chain. The first buy is quoted with the SDK's curve math, so the minimum output is derived from the real curve, not a guess.

Trade a curve

Buy into or sell out of any active Ammora Launch Curve. Quotes come from the SDK's on-chain curve state (segmented math, live fee) and the swap is planned + simulated before your wallet signs.

Graduate a curve

When a curve's quote threshold is filled it becomes Filled — anyone can then migrate it into its canonical AMM pool (concentrated graduation, migration mode 0). This tab shows the live progress and performs the migration.
